# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 2026**

## Data Collection
DOCSIOS does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to third parties. The app does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Network Communication
DOCSIOS communicates exclusively with the DOCSight backend instance configured by the user. All requests are made directly to your self-hosted backend. No data is routed through external servers operated by the developer.

## Diagnostics
DOCSIOS uses Apple's MetricKit framework to monitor app performance and stability. All diagnostic data remains on your device and is only shared if you explicitly export or report it.

## Credentials
Your backend URL and API token are stored locally on your device using the iOS Keychain. They are never transmitted to anyone other than your configured DOCSight backend.

## Changes
This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact
